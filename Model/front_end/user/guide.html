    • 🧭 User Guide

    Welcome to the Comic Character Generator! This guide will help you understand how to use the platform to create and manage comic-style character images with ease.

    🔹 Getting Started

    To begin generating characters, follow these steps:

    • Click the "Set Player Description" button on the homepage.
    • Fill in the basic character traits such as Name, Gender, Hair, Eyes, Clothing Style, and any special features.
    • Click "Generate Image" to preview your character.
    • You can tweak the options and regenerate until you’re happy with the result.
    📝 Tip: Only Name and Age are free text inputs — the rest are dropdowns to help you get quick and consistent results.

    🕘 History

    All your previously generated characters are automatically saved to your browser's local storage.

    • You can access the history via the History page.
    • There, you can delete individual characters or clear the entire history.

    ⚙️ Account Settings

    Use the Settings page to:

    • Update your Name, Email, or Password.
    • Securely update your credentials with proper confirmation fields.
    • Permanently delete your account from the Danger Zone section.

    📌 Notes

    • This platform uses your browser's local storage for saving history — clearing your cache or using a different device will reset it.
    • More features like downloading images, exporting character sheets, and sharing to social platforms are coming soon!
    ✨ Stay creative and keep generating awesome comic characters!
